How to play

Desktop
  • Click look around · W A S D swim / walk
  • Space up · C down · Shift sprint
  • Left mouse use your tool · 1–8 / wheel pick a tool
  • E interact / enter or leave a vehicle
  • I inventory · Tab PDA · F helmet lamp
  • R rotate a building piece · Esc cancel
Meta Quest (VR) — gorilla movement
  • No joystick: slap the ground with your hands and pull back to run
  • Push off hard and let go to launch · pull strokes to swim
  • Punch resources to break them
  • Right stick turn · right trigger use tool · A / B tools
  • X PDA · Y interact / vehicle · left stick click lamp

You start in the dive station lobby. Walk to the computer to play alone, host or join a room with a code, or change your gorilla's name, colour and turning.

The story so far

The colony ship Odyssey-7 was hit by an unknown energy discharge in orbit of the ocean world Nerathis-9. As it falls through the sky, the crew scramble for the escape pods.

You wake up in Escape Pod 3, bobbing on an endless alien sea. The Odyssey-7 has crashed on the horizon, and its cracked reactor won't hold for long.

Gather resources, craft tools, build a base, survive the creatures that hunt these waters, and uncover what brought you down — then find a way off this planet.
